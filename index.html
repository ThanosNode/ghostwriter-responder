FAQ

Common questions

How does the free trial work? +

Your free trial starts automatically the moment you complete checkout — 14 days on Monthly, 30 days on Annual. Your card is on file but you won't be charged until the trial ends. Cancel before then and you pay absolutely nothing.

Does it work on both Google Business and Yelp? +

Yes — GhostWriter injects a "Draft Smart Reply" button into both Google Business and Yelp review pages automatically. One extension covers both platforms with zero extra setup.

Will the replies sound like a robot? +

No. GhostWriter uses Anthropic's Claude AI which produces genuinely human-sounding, empathetic responses. You also choose your brand tone — Professional, Friendly, Luxury, Casual, or Formal — so every reply feels consistent with your voice.

Does it post replies automatically? +

Never. GhostWriter fills the reply box for you to review — you always have final approval before anything gets posted to your profile. You stay in complete control.

What is the difference between Monthly and Annual? +

Both plans include identical features. Annual is billed once at $60/year — that's $5/month, saving you $12 vs monthly billing. Annual also comes with a longer 30-day free trial compared to 14 days on Monthly.

How do I cancel? +

Log into your Stripe Customer Portal via the link in the footer below. Cancel in two clicks — your subscription ends at the current billing period with no fees and no questions asked.
